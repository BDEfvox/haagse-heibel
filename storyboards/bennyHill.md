# bennyHill: The chase that goes round and round

A thief with a swag bag, a cop, a dog, a chef and another cop chase each other in a loop until nobody knows who is chasing whom.

- **Cast and props:** robber with money bag, two cops, a dog, a chef.
- **Needs at the spot:** Both pavements and the road between them open (the loop of bennyLoop).

| Beat | What happens | Must be seen | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 the parade | They come past in single file: robber laughing with the bag, cop, dog, chef waving a ladle, second cop. | the whole line | high shot of the loop |
| 2 reveal | Close on the robber running, laughing over his shoulder. Title card DE ACHTERVOLGING. | robber's face + bag | tracking close, hand-held |
| 3 round again | They cross the road and come back along the far pavement; the order has changed (the dog now leads). | the whole line | top/high shot |
| 4 the stop | The robber stops dead and steps aside. Everyone else piles into each other: a heap, stars. | the pile-up | dutch, freeze |
| 5 the tiptoe | The robber tiptoes away the other way, finger to lips, then laughs. | robber | tracking |
| 6 the heap wakes | The heap untangles, points, and sets off after him in the opposite direction, the dog now carrying the bag. | the line, reversed | wide |
