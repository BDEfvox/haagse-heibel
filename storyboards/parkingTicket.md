# parkingTicket: The parking fine

A traffic warden writes the ticket of a lifetime; the owner arrives one second too late and makes it worse.

**Setup, trigger, payoff.** Setup: a warden strolls in along the parked cars, peers at a windscreen and checks his watch. Trigger: he writes the ticket and slaps it under the wiper (ka-ching). Payoff: the owner comes out of his real front door (or runs up the street) one second too late, pleads, and gets a second ticket for his trouble.

- **Cast and props:** warden (cop, strolling in from out of the frame, either way along the street), car owner (a civilian or an oma, never one of the district's extras), the parked car (one of the real parked cars). The owner comes out of the real door of the nearest home (`T.doorway` via `resident`: the door opens on a dark doorway and he steps out of it) or, with no door in reach, runs in from out of the frame.
- **Needs at the spot:** A parked car at the spot.
