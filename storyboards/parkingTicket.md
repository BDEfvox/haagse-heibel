# parkingTicket: The parking fine

A traffic warden writes the ticket of a lifetime; the owner arrives one second too late and makes it worse.

- **Cast and props:** warden (cop), car owner, the parked car (one of the real parked cars).
- **Needs at the spot:** A parked car at the spot.

| Beat | What happens | Must be seen | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 the patrol | The warden strolls along the parked cars, pad in hand, peers at a windscreen, checks a watch. | warden + car | tracking |
| 2 reveal | Smug look to camera, pen clicked. Title card DE BOA. | warden's face | hero, freeze |
| 3 the ticket | Writes with a flourish, tears it off, slaps it under the wiper: BON! €109. Ka-ching. | warden + the ticket on the windscreen | close at bonnet height |
| 4 the owner | The owner bursts out (door, or running up with shopping): NEEE! IK STA ER NET! Skids to a stop. | owner | crash zoom |
| 5 the plea | Owner pleads, hands together, points at the watch. The warden shakes his head slowly. Owner drops to his knees. | both faces | orbit / two-shot |
| 6 the second ticket | The warden looks down at the kneeling owner, writes a second ticket ("blocking the pavement") and hands it over. The owner faints flat on his back. | both | dutch, then ground level on the fainted owner |
| 7 epilogue | The warden strolls off whistling, strut in his step. The owner sits up, both tickets in hand, sighs. | warden walking away, owner | wide |
