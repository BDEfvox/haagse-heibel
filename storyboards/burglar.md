# burglar: Burglary: in through the window, out with the swag bag

A burglar lets himself in through a first-floor window, comes out with a bulging € bag, gloats, and runs straight into the law (or gets clean away).

- **Cast and props:** burglar (robber), owner in a nightcap, cop, a cat. Props: crowbar, balcony, vase, money bag.
- **Needs at the spot:** A real house front with a window, a clear landing under it and 9 m of clear pavement.

| Beat | What happens | Must be seen | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 casing | He creeps along the fronts on tiptoe with a crowbar, stops under the window, looks up. | burglar | wide establishing, then track alongside |
| 2 reveal | He turns to us, grins, rubs his hands, sniggers. Title card DE INBREKER. | burglar's face | hero (low, tilted), freeze |
| 3 the climb | A leap onto the balcony, the crowbar cracks the frame, he slips inside. | burglar + window | medium on house, both in frame |
| 4 inside | The light flickers; rummaging noises; a vase flies out and smashes; the cat flies out, lands, stalks off. | window | push-in on the window |
| 4b the loot | He is back on the balcony holding a bulging € bag: HEBBES! | burglar + bag at window | same |
| 4c the bag | The bag goes over the railing, hits the pavement, bounces, banknotes puff out. | the bag | ground level on the bag |
| 5 the gloat | He jumps down, slings the bag over his shoulder and laughs out loud (HA HA HA). | burglar's face and the bag | hero from his bag side |
| 6 the owner | The light goes on: the owner on the balcony, double take, MIJN SPAARGELD! The alarm starts. The burglar gets a fright. | burglar + owner | medium on both |
