# fenderBender: The fender bender: a pigeon, three cars and one form

A pigeon strolls into the lane, car A stops for it, car B (radio loud) does not, and a third car joins in. The drivers argue, fence with bumpers, chase a gust-blown damage form, and the pigeon walks through it all.

- **Cast and props:** pigeon, cars A, B and a third car C, drivers 1, 2 and 3, a cop (level 3+), bystanders filming, police cars (level 4+). Props: two bumpers, a damage form (paper), steam.
- **Needs at the spot:** A two-lane road with at least 44 m of clear tarmac in both lanes; the road is closed to traffic for the duration.
