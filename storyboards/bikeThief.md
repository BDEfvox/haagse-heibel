# bikeThief: Bike theft: the wrong bike first, then the right one, then the fall

A thief with bolt cutters snips the child's bike free first and finds it far too small, snips the lock of the real bike and rides off gloating over his shoulder at the owner, until the one thing the viewer has been shown on the street stops the bike dead and sends him over the handlebars.

- **Setup / trigger / payoff (three sentences):** Setup: a rack, a child's bike and a real bike, both on cheap locks, and a thief with cutters (a lamp post ahead of him, or a second padlocked chain, is planted in a shot of its own). Trigger: he rides off looking back at the owner and the planted thing stops the bike. Payoff: he sits in the street seeing stars with the cause in frame, and the owner takes the bikes home.
- **The fall has one of three causes, chosen by `ctx.rnd` (`flip` names `fallChain`, `fallLamp`; `kid` is the default):**
  - `chain`: a second, thick yellow chain is padlocked round the rack bar and through the real bike's rear wheel. Plant: close ground shot (4a) of the chain and padlock while he is already on the bike. Fall: the chain pays out behind him, goes bar-taut at the end of its 5 m, the bike stops dead (ZING), he flies. Afterwards the chain is still taut round the rack (6c), and the owner unlocks it before taking the bike home.
  - `lamp`: a real lamp post from `L.lamps` that stands on his pavement 5 to 11 m along the getaway. Plant: it is in the establishing wide shot (1) and again in a plant shot (4a). Fall: he looks back over his shoulder, laughing at the owner, and rides into it (KLONG).
  - `kid`: the child's bike he threw down in beat 3d lies across his lane (it is knocked over and turned across the lane in 3d, and shown again in 4a). Fall: the front wheel hits it (KLETS) and he is catapulted over it.
- **Cast and props:** thief (raccoon or robber), the bike's owner (balcony, shop, bench or passer-by: whatever the spot has; `resident`), a cop at higher levels (or `flip` `cop`). Props: bolt cutters, the child's pink bike, the real bike, two thin cheap locks (cut), in the chain version the thick padlocked chain.
- **Needs at the spot:** a bike rack with 10 m of clear pavement to ride along (`stage.pave far 10 m`, anchor rack). Both bikes stand at the rack when the scene starts; nobody rolls in alone and the bikes leave only in the owner's hands.
- **Pacing:** The walk-in of the thief is the walk past the rack (tight opening shot). The owner is on his way from the moment the real lock falls, so nobody is waited for; the epilogue is short and the rig walks the rest off.
