# bikeThief: Bike theft: the wrong bike first, then the right one, then the chain

A thief with bolt cutters snips the wrong lock first and rides off on a child's bike, gets shouted at, snips the right one and makes his getaway, until the discarded child's bike hooks onto the dragging chain and catapults him into the street.

- **Cast and props:** thief (robber or raccoon), the bike's owner (balcony, shop, bench or passer-by, whatever the spot has), a second person who rushes in, a cop at higher levels. Props: bolt cutters, the child's pink bike, the real bike, the cut chain.
- **Needs at the spot:** A bike rack with at least 10 m of clear pavement along it to ride away.
