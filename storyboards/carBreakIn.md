# carBreakIn: Car break-in: the optimist who tries every handle

A thief tries every door handle down a row of parked cars, sets off an alarm, acts natural, and finally smashes the window of the one car with a sleeper in the back seat. The loot flies, the owner wakes, and the chase ends in either a bust or a smug getaway.

- **Cast and props:** thief (robber or raccoon), the sleeping owner in a nightcap, a nosy bystander (when one is available), a cop (level 4+). Props: crowbar, radio, shoe, wallet, car alarm lights, handcuffs.
- **Needs at the spot:** A parked car with at least 8 m of clear pavement ahead of it and 6 m behind it.
