# pickpocket: Pickpocketing

A pickpocket lifts a wallet from a passer-by's back pocket, gloats over the takings (a moth and a loyalty card) and is caught by the victim he underestimated.

- **Cast and props:** pickpocket (robber or hoodie), the victim (a passer-by borrowed from the crowd), a cop.
- **Needs at the spot:** A passer-by with 9 m of pavement ahead (needPed).

| Beat | What happens | Must be seen | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 the mark | The victim strolls along, a fat bright wallet sticking out of the back pocket. The pickpocket falls in behind on tiptoe. | victim (wallet visible) + pickpocket | tracking, both in frame |
| 2 reveal | He turns to us, wiggles his fingers, sly grin. Title card DE ZAKKENROLLER. | pickpocket's face | hero, freeze |
| 3 the lift | Close on the pocket: his hand reaches, the wallet slides out. YOINK. The victim walks on whistling. | the wallet and the hand | ground/close on the pocket, hip height |
| 4 the gloat | He stops, opens the wallet, sniggers... a moth flies out, then one receipt. His face falls. Sad trombone. | pickpocket's face + wallet | face shot |
| 5 the pat | The victim stops, pats pockets, freezes, turns: MIJN PORTEMONNEE! Points: DIEF! | victim's face | crash zoom |
| 6 the chase | He bolts; the victim sprints after him. He looks back laughing and runs into a cop's raised hand (or slips): tumble. The wallet arcs through the air back into the victim's hand. | pickpocket + pursuer; then the wallet in the air | tracking, then dutch at the fall |
| 7 epilogue | Cop cuffs him (stars round his head); the victim counts the loyalty cards, satisfied, and walks on. | all three | two-shot, then wide |
