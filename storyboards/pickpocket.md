# pickpocket: Pickpocketing

A pickpocket lifts a wallet from a passer-by's back pocket, gloats over the takings (a moth, a rolling coin or a very long receipt) and is caught when he runs, laughing over his shoulder, into the cop who was seen standing ahead of him.

- **Setup / trigger / payoff (three sentences):** Setup: a victim strolls along whistling with a fat bright wallet sticking out of his pocket, a pickpocket creeps up behind him, and (planted ahead in the chase shot) a cop stands further down the pavement. Trigger: the victim pats his pockets, the thief bolts laughing over his shoulder with the victim after him. Payoff: he runs into the cop's raised hand, the wallet arcs back into the victim's hand, the cop cuffs him and the victim counts his loyalty cards.
- **Cast and props:** pickpocket (robber or hoodie), the victim (a passer-by borrowed from the crowd with `takePed`, no waiting; on a quiet street an invented one, `extraKind`, who walks in from out of the frame while the first shot holds the place he is coming to), a cop who walks in from out of the frame during beat 5 (nobody pops in). Props: the fat wallet, and what is in it: chosen by `ctx.rnd` out of a moth, one coin that rolls away, a very long receipt (always followed by a receipt).
- **Needs at the spot:** a strip of pavement 10 m long, 1.2 m deep (`stage.pave any 10 m`).

| Beat | What happens | Must be seen | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 the mark | The victim strolls along, a fat bright wallet sticking out of the back pocket (an invented victim walks in first). | victim (wallet visible) | tracking, or a wide on his entrance |
| 1b the tail | The pickpocket walks in from out of the frame behind him and falls in on tiptoe. | victim + pickpocket | tracking, both in frame |
| 2 reveal | He turns to us, wiggles his fingers, sly grin. Title card DE ZAKKENROLLER. | pickpocket's face | hero, freeze |
| 3 the lift | Close on the pocket: his hand reaches, the wallet slides out. YOINK. The victim walks on whistling. | the wallet and the hand | close on the pocket, hip height |
| 4 the gloat | He opens the wallet, sniggers: a moth flies out / a coin rolls away / then a receipt. His face falls. Sad trombone. | pickpocket's face + wallet | face shot |
| 5 the pat | The victim stops, pats pockets, freezes, turns: MIJN PORTEMONNEE! (The cop walks in meanwhile, out of the picture.) | victim's face | crash zoom |
| 6 the chase | He bolts, the victim sprints after him. The cop stands ahead of them, in frame. He looks back laughing. | pickpocket + pursuer + the cop ahead | tracking |
| 6b the fall | He runs into the cop's raised hand: BONK, tumble. | pickpocket + cop | dutch |
| 6c the wallet flies back | The wallet arcs through the air into the victim's hand. | wallet + victim | medium |
| 7 epilogue | Cop cuffs him (stars round his head). | cop + thief | two-shot |
| 7b the victim | The victim counts the loyalty cards, satisfied, and walks on while the culprit is led off. | all three | wide |
