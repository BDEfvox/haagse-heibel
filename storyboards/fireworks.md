# fireworks: Illegal fireworks

A lad with a rocket bigger than his sense, a bin, and a fuse that seems to have gone out.

- **Cast and props:** the firework lad (hoodie), two mates filming, a cop.
- **Needs at the spot:** A litter bin at the spot.

| Beat | What happens | Must be seen | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 the plan | He sneaks to the bin carrying a huge rocket; two mates follow with phones up. | lad with rocket + bin | tracking |
| 2 reveal | Grin to camera, rocket held like a trophy. Title card DE VUURWERKBENDE. | lad's face + rocket | hero, freeze |
| 3 the fuse | He plants it in the bin and lights the fuse (sparks). Everyone backs off, fingers in ears, crouching. | the fuse sparkling, then all three covering ears | ground-level close on the bin, then wide |
| 4 the fizzle | ...nothing. The sparks die. Pfff. He creeps back, leans right over the bin to look. | lad leaning over the bin | dolly in slowly |
| 5 WHOOSH | The rocket launches past his nose; the bin lid flies off spinning; high above: PANG, then two more bursts lighting everything. | lad's shocked face, then the sky burst | crash zoom on his face, then a low shot looking up |
| 6 the lid | The bin lid comes back down on his head like a hat: BONK. Stars. The mates fall about laughing. | lad with the lid on his head; mates laughing | close, then two-shot of the mates |
| 7 the law | A cop arrives; the mates scatter; the lad, lid over his eyes, walks straight into the cop. The cop lifts the lid: MEEKOMEN. | lad + cop | two-shot, then wide |
