# fireworks: Illegal fireworks

A lad with a rocket bigger than his sense, a bin, a lighter, and a fuse that seems to have gone out.

- **Setup / trigger / payoff (three sentences):** Setup: the lad carries a huge rocket in from out of the frame, plants it in the bin pointing straight up, lights the fuse with his lighter (the sparks are shown) and everybody backs off, the passers-by about backing off with them. Trigger: it fizzles, so the crowd and the lad creep back and he leans right over the bin: it goes off, the rocket climbs out of the bin (the camera follows it up) and bursts, the lid flies off. Payoff: the lid comes down on somebody's head and the cop walks into the picture and meets the one with the lid over his eyes.
- **Cast and props:** the firework lad (hoodie), two mates filming (all three walk in from out of the frame), a cop who walks in from out of the frame while the lid is in the air (nobody pops in), passers-by who flee and come back (`ctx.stageMode('flee')` at the lighting and the whoosh, `'watch'` during the fizzle and after the bursts). Props: the rocket, a lighter with a flame (out of his pocket), the bin lid. Whoever the lid lands on varies (`flip` `mateLid`: the lad, or one of his mates).
- **Needs at the spot:** a litter bin and 12 m of pavement round it (`stage.pave any 12 m`, anchor bin); the bin nearest the stage centre is used.
- **Pacing:** Tight opening on the bin: the lad and his mates walk in together within about two seconds; the fizzle beat is trimmed and the scene ends on the culprit being led off, the camera lets go there.
