# plofkraak: The cash-machine blast

Two men in striped shirts blow up a cash machine, dance in the rain of banknotes, fill their bags laughing and leave in a van, seconds before the police arrive.

- **Cast and props:** two robbers, van, a few opportunist bystanders, a police car and a cop. Props: bomb with fuse, two money bags, the machine's front panel.
- **Needs at the spot:** A cash machine at the spot (ctx.L.atm, not blown yet). Road closed for the duration; works on a pedestrian street too (the van uses the pavement side).
