# scuffle: The scuffle: two egos, one pavement, one dust cloud

**Setup:** two ordinary strangers (both plain pedestrians, never one of the district types) walk in from opposite ends of the pavement, each absorbed in his own business. **Trigger:** something happens that the viewer SEES before anybody is angry (one of three, picked by `ctx.rnd`; force with `flip` names `shoes` / `note`): the two cannot get past each other and shoulders bump, a coffee flies out of a hand and lands on a shirt and the pavement (`coffee`); a boot lands on the toe of a brand-new white trainer and leaves a footprint (`shoes`); a banknote blows along the pavement and both go for it and tear it in half (`note`). **Payoff:** a word, a shove, the cartoon dust cloud with a granny taking bets, two dazed men; then either the law makes them shake hands, or they find out they are mates and walk off together (`mates`).

- **Cast and props:** two civ pedestrians, a granny bookie with a betting slip, one or two onlookers (one films with a phone), a cop (a second cop and a police car at high levels, not in the `mates` ending). Props per cause: coffee cup and puddle (`fx.mark`) and a stain on his shirt; white sneakers on his feet and a dirty footprint; a glowing banknote that rips in two. Always: flying shoes, the dust cloud.
- **Needs at the spot:** `stage: pave { side: any, len: 10, depth: 1.6 }`: 10 m of clear pavement line, clear ground beside it for the ring of onlookers. Centred on `ctx.stage.x`.
- **Pacing:** The men walk in one after the other, each from his own end of the pavement in a tight shot of his own (beats 1 and 1b); the cop starts running in while the two sit dazed, so he is there on cue; the rig walks everybody off.
- **Entrances:** both men and the granny walk in from outside the frame (`R.enter`, after the shot is up); nobody pops.
