# scuffle: The scuffle: two egos, one pavement, one dust cloud

Two strangers bump on the pavement, trade words nose to nose while a crowd gathers and a granny takes bets, vanish into a rolling cartoon dust cloud, and come out dazed and sheepish; the law makes them shake hands.

- **Cast and props:** two pedestrians (one maybe a chef), a granny bookie with a betting slip, one or two onlookers (one films with a phone), a cop (a second cop and a police car at high levels). Props: phone, wafel, flying shoes, the dust cloud, a shoe in a dazed hand.
- **Needs at the spot:** 8 m of clear pavement line (room for the cloud to roll 7+ m one way) and clear ground beside it for the ring of onlookers.
