# shopHeist: Shoplifting: the cart of goodies

A "customer" fills a shopping cart with the shop's goods, walks a television through the security gate and tries to wheel the whole leaning tower away, until it comes down on him. The shopkeeper sends him the bill.

**Setup, trigger, payoff.** Setup: a shopkeeper sweeps in front of his shop (the shop's own sign over the door) while a "customer" eyes a shopping cart. Trigger: the customer goes in and out of the real shop door with a box, then a television: the security gate beeps and the keeper turns round. Payoff: the getaway with the leaning tower of goods, which comes down on him; the broom sweeps it all back through the shop door, and the bill.

- **Cast and props:** thief (robber), shopkeeper (chef) with a broom, two onlookers (one films), a cop. Props: security gate with red lights, shopping cart, loot boxes, television with its tag, a box on the head.
- **Doors:** the shop door is the real door of a real shop unit (`L.shops`, a parody name on its sign); the thief goes in and comes out of it, the keeper steps out of it and goes back in (`T.doorway`: it opens on a dark doorway with a leaf of the door's own colour and the glass in it). The cart is wheeled back in through it at the end.
- **Needs at the spot:** A real shop door (not a bank) and 9 m or more of clear pavement along it to run. `stage: { pave: far 10 x 2, anchor: 'shop' }`.
