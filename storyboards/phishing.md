# phishing: Phishing, but make it literal

A hooded phisher unfolds a desk on the pavement, sends a text, fishes a wallet off a delighted victim with a rod and line, reels the money in to a pile on his desk, gets greedy, ties himself up in two more lines and loses it all.

- **Cast and props:** hooded phisher, a phone-zombie victim, two more victims, one or two filming onlookers, a cyber cop on a bike (level 3+). Props: folding desk with laptop, fishing rod, glowing hook and lines, wallet, wads of cash, a money bag.
- **Needs at the spot:** a clear 1.2 m patch on each pavement (his, and his victims') with 14 m of room.
