# carChase: Reckless driving: the chase

A speeder tears down the street, slaloms, pulls a handbrake turn in a cloud of tyre smoke and comes back the other way, with the police doing it rather less gracefully.

- **Cast and props:** speeder's hatchback (driver: hoodie), two police cars, two cops on foot at the end. Props: a hubcap, a ticket pad.
- **Needs at the spot:** A real road along the whole stretch (−22..22), half-width ≥ 2.2. Road closed to traffic for the duration.

| Beat | What happens | Must be seen | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 roar in | Engine note; the car comes straight at the lens from the far end, nose lifting, weaving a little. Title card DE SNELHEIDSDUIVEL. | the car | nose shot (long lens, bumper height) |
| 2 the slalom | It swerves left-right down the street, the body rolling hard over its wheels, tyres squealing, smoke off the back wheels. A police car appears behind, siren on. | the car (roll visible), then car + police | chase cam alongside, hand-held |
| 3 the handbrake | It yanks the handbrake: the tail swings round 180°, the body leaning right over, black marks and smoke all the way round. Held frame at full lean. | the car, side-on | ground level, close; freeze |
| 4 the pass | It squats and launches back the way it came, passing the police car nose to nose. The police car stands on its brakes (nose dives): HÈ?! | both cars | high shot of the road |
| 5 the police turn | The police car tries the same turn: overshoots, bumps the kerb (jolt), a hubcap rolls away down the street. It gives chase. | police car, then the hubcap | dutch on the police car, ground on the hubcap |
| 6 boxed in | A second police car comes head-on from the other end. The speeder brakes hard (nose right down, smoke) and stops between the two, a hand's width from each. | all three cars | wide, slightly high; then ground level on the gap |
| 7a the ticket | The driver gets out, hands up, sheepish. A cop writes with a flourish and slaps the ticket on the windscreen: BON! €1.249. Sad trombone. | driver + cop | two-shot, then close on driver's face |
| 7b the gap | (other ending) He grins, finds the gap, squirts through between the police cars; they lurch forward and bump noses: BONK. He is gone, laughing. | the speeder, then the two police cars | chase cam, then dutch on the bump |
| 8 epilogue | Police cars leave (or sit steaming). The street is quiet; a single hubcap wobbles to a stop. | the hubcap / the scene | wide |
