# seagullFries: The gull that steals the fries

A customer is enjoying a cone of chips when a gull circles, a wingman distracts, and the scout dives in slow motion. A tug of war, a smug gull, a stampede of seagulls, and in the end a pigeon gets the last chip.

- **Cast and props:** a passer-by (borrowed from the crowd), a scout gull, a wingman gull, a flock, a pigeon, a cop with a stroopwafel (level 4+). Props: cone of fries, fallen chips, stroopwafel.
- **Needs at the spot:** A pedestrian and room to run (the scene finds its own).
