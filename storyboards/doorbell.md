# doorbell: Belletje trek (neighbour rows)

Two kids ring a doorbell and run. They cannot resist doing it twice.

- **Cast and props:** two kids, the resident (granny or a man in a dressing gown).
- **Needs at the spot:** A real front door (not a shop) and a hiding spot 5–7 m along the pavement.

| Beat | What happens | Must be seen | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 the dare | Two kids creep along the fronts; one pushes the other forward; giggles. | both kids | two-shot tracking |
| 2 reveal | The brave one grins at us. Title card BELLETJE TREK. | kid's face | hero, freeze |
| 3 the ring | On tiptoe, stretching up, finger on the bell: DING DONG! | kid + door | close on the door, low |
| 4 the run | Both bolt, laughing HA HA, and crouch behind a parked car / bin / corner, peeking out. | both kids running, then hiding | tracking, then low on the hiding place |
| 5 the door | The door opens: the resident looks left, right, up. ?! Shrugs. Goes back in. | resident | medium on the door |
| 6 again | The kid creeps back, grinning, reaches for the bell... the door flies open first: the resident was waiting, with a bucket. SPLASH. The kid stands dripping, eyes wide. | kid + resident + the water | medium, then crash zoom on the dripping kid |
| 7 the last laugh | The resident laughs (HA HA HA), dusts hands, shuts the door. The other kid laughs too. The wet one squelches off. | resident laughing, then both kids | close on resident, then wide |
