# graffiti: Vandalism: the tag, the critic and the puddle

A tagger sprays "070" on a real wall (or transformer box) while his lookout whistles; a passing critic gives it one star and "improves" it with a gold underline; the cop who comes to arrest somebody slips in the wet paint, or arrests the critic.

- **Setup / trigger / payoff (three sentences):** Setup: the tagger sprays the tag on the wall (`canvasFor`: a transformer box or a plain house wall), the drips run into a puddle and he steps back through it, leaving wet footprints, while the lookout looks out along the street the other way. Trigger: the critic takes the can to add a gold underline, and the cop walks in from behind the lookout's back; the kid and the lookout run off along the street leaving a trail of prints. Payoff (one of two, `flip` `critic`): the cop follows the prints to the tag and slides into the puddle; or he finds the critic still holding the can next to the fresh underline and arrests him.
- **Cast and props:** tagger and lookout (hoodies), a critic (a passer-by borrowed from the crowd, or an invented extra), a cop who walks in from out of the frame on the side the lookout is NOT watching (he starts walking during the improvement, so nobody pops in). Props: spray can, the tag (glowing pixels), drips, a puddle of paint, wet footprints, the critic's gold underline.
- **Needs at the spot:** a transformer box or a plain house wall (no shop sign, clear of door and window) with pavement in front, 10 m of pavement (`stage.pave any 10 m`); the wall nearest the stage's centre is used.
- **Pacing:** The establishing shot is tight on the wall, the lookout and the tagger walk in together within two seconds; the last beat fades the footprints while the camera takes a short last look, then lets go and the rig walks the cop (and critic) off.
