# graffiti: Vandalism: the tag, the critic and the puddle

A tagger sprays "070" on a wall while his lookout whistles; a passing critic gives it one star and "improves" it with a gold underline; the cop who comes to arrest everybody slips in the wet paint.

- **Cast and props:** tagger and lookout (hoodies), a critic (a passer-by), a cop. Props: spray can, the tag (glowing pixels), drips, a puddle of paint, wet footprints, the critic's gold underline.
- **Needs at the spot:** A transformer box or a plain house wall (no shop sign, clear of door and window) with pavement in front.
