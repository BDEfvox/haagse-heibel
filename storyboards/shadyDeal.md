# shadyDeal: The street deal

A man in a long coat sells 'oregano' from inside it, until the law sniffs the air.

- **Cast and props:** dealer (hoodie, long coat), a customer (borrowed passer-by), a cop.
- **Needs at the spot:** A clear spot on the camera-side pavement and a passer-by.

| Beat | What happens | Must be seen | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 loitering | The dealer leans by a lamp post, looking up and down the street, far too casual. | dealer | dolly in |
| 2 reveal | He opens one side of his coat: rows of little glowing jars. Sly grin. Title card DE KRUIDENMAN. | dealer's face + the jars | hero, freeze |
| 3 the customer | Psst. A passer-by stops, peers, sniffs a jar: HATSJOE! A green cloud. Money changes hands (a wad passes over). | both + the jar / the wad | two-shot, then close on the handover |
| 4 the law | A cop strolls up behind the dealer. The customer sees him first: eyes wide, points. The dealer turns, snaps the coat shut, whistles at the sky. | cop + dealer + customer | two-shot with the cop in the background, then crash zoom on the dealer |
| 5 the tap | The cop sniffs, taps the coat with one finger. A jar drops out. Then another. Then all of them, clattering. | the jars on the pavement, dealer's face | ground level on the jars, then face |
| 6 the run | The dealer runs; the cop gives chase; the dealer skids on his own jar and goes down in a green cloud; the cop sneezes too. | dealer's fall + cop | tracking, then dutch |
| 7 epilogue | Cop leads him off. The customer picks up one last jar, shrugs, pockets it and walks on. | customer | close, then wide |
