# herringHeist: The herring heist

A dog steals a herring from the stall. Then somebody steals it from the dog.

- **Cast and props:** stall keeper (chef), a dog, a gull.
- **Needs at the spot:** A market stall at the spot.

| Beat | What happens | Must be seen | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 the stall | The keeper arranges the herring proudly. A dog sits beside the stall, tail wagging, staring. | keeper + dog + stall | wide on the stall |
| 2 reveal | Close on the dog: innocent face, tail going. Title card DE HARINGDIEF. | the dog | low close, freeze |
| 3 the grab | The dog barks at something behind the keeper; the keeper turns to look; the dog leaps, herring in mouth: YOINK. | dog + herring | medium, low |
| 4 the chase | The dog trots off, head high, fish flapping. The keeper gives chase waving a ladle, slips on a dropped fish and goes flying. | dog with the fish, then the keeper's fall | tracking, then dutch |
| 5 the gull | A gull drops out of the sky and takes the herring straight from the dog's mouth. The dog stops. Looks up. The keeper, sitting, looks up too. | gull + dog at the swap, then both looking up | close on the swap, then two-shot |
| 6 epilogue | Sad trombone. The keeper pats the dog and gives it a small fish from his pocket. They walk back to the stall together. | keeper + dog | wide |
