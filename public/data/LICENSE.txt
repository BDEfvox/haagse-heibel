Data licences
=============

The files in this folder are derived data, simplified, combined and reformatted for this site.

- Streets, land cover, water, rails, trees and points of interest:
  (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0.
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
  The OpenStreetMap-derived parts of these files are made available under the ODbL.

- Building outlines, heights, roof types and construction years:
  (c) 3DBAG by tudelft3d and 3DGI, CC BY 4.0. https://docs.3dbag.nl/en/copyright/
  Changes: footprints simplified, attributes reduced, heights aggregated for the city overview.

- Terrain heights (world/city-terrain.png): AHN (Actueel Hoogtebestand Nederland) DTM 0.5 m, Rijkswaterstaat, CC0 1.0,
  via PDOK WCS. https://service.pdok.nl/rws/ahn/wcs/v1_0
  Changes: resampled to 8 m cells, holes filled, smoothed, sea set to 0 m, encoded in 8 bits.

- Municipal trees (the tree positions in world/tiles that do not come from OpenStreetMap): Gemeente Den Haag tree register
  ("Bomen met ID", 123,603 points, last edited November 2022), published on the municipality's open-data portal
  (Opendata Portaal Den Haag), https://services1.arcgis.com/sbwSZYQOs4GMs7tr/arcgis/rest/services/Bomen_met_ID/FeatureServer/0
  The layer itself carries no licence statement; the municipality's matching "Bomen" dataset on data.overheid.nl
  (https://data.overheid.nl/dataset/bomen-gpkg) is published under CC0 1.0. Source: Gemeente 's-Gravenhage.
  Changes: positions only, rounded to decimetres, trees within 2.5 m of an OpenStreetMap tree dropped.

- Neighbourhood borders and residents: CBS Wijken en buurten 2025 via PDOK, CC BY 4.0. Source: CBS.

- Registered crime per neighbourhood: Politie open data via CBS (table 47018NED). Source: Politie / CBS.

Use of these sources does not imply endorsement by any of them.
