Data licences

The files in this folder are derived data, simplified, combined and reformatted for this site.

- Streets, land cover, water, rails, trees and points of interest:
  (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0.
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
  The OpenStreetMap-derived parts of these files are made available under the ODbL.

- Building outlines, heights, roof types and construction years:
  (c) 3DBAG by tudelft3d and 3DGI, CC BY 4.0. https://docs.3dbag.nl/en/copyright/
  Changes: footprints simplified, attributes reduced, heights aggregated for the city overview.

- Neighbourhood borders and residents: CBS Wijken en buurten 2025 via PDOK, CC BY 4.0. Source: CBS.

- Registered crime per neighbourhood: Politie open data via CBS (table 47018NED). Source: Politie / CBS.
